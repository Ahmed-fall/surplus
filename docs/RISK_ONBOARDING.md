# Risk Onboarding Copy for R5

## Three onboarding questions

1. How steady is your monthly PayPal income?
   - Very steady – similar amounts each month from retainers or recurring clients.
   - Somewhat variable – income changes month to month but stays positive.
   - Highly variable – large swings, seasonal spikes, or project-based work.

2. How urgent are your fixed monthly costs?
   - Low – I can cover 6+ months of costs from cash without stress.
   - Medium – 3-6 months of costs would feel safe.
   - High – I need 3 months or less buffer to stay comfortable.

3. What is your comfort with market exposure for idle cash?
   - Low – I prefer capital preservation and short-term stability.
   - Medium – I accept some ups and downs for modest growth.
   - High – I am comfortable with longer-term market exposure for potential growth.

## Classification guidance

* Most answers “Very steady / Low / Low” → CONSERVATIVE
* Mixed answers with some variability → BALANCED
* Most answers “Highly variable / High / High” → GROWTH

This guidance is educational only. The engine computes the actual risk profile impact.
